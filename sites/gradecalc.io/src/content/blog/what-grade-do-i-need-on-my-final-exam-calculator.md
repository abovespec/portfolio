# What Grade Do I Need on My Final? Exam Calculator

Final exam season brings a specific anxiety: "What grade do I need on my final to get an A?" or "Can I still pass if I bomb the final?" This guide shows the exact math and provides a calculator to find your answer in seconds.

## The Formula

**Final Exam Grade Needed = (Target Grade - (Current Grade x (1 - Final Weight))) / Final Weight**

## Example Calculation

Your current grade is 82%. The final is worth 30% of your grade. You want a B (80%) in the class.

- Target: 80
- Current: 82
- Final weight: 0.30
- Current weight: 0.70

Current contribution: 82 x 0.70 = 57.4
Points needed from final: 80 - 57.4 = 22.6
Final grade needed: 22.6 / 0.30 = **75.3%**

You need a 75.3% on the final to finish with an 80%.

## Another Example: The A-Goal

Current grade: 88%. Final worth 25%. Target: 90%.

- Current contribution: 88 x 0.75 = 66
- Points needed: 90 - 66 = 24
- Final grade needed: 24 / 0.25 = **96%**

You need a 96% on the final. That is tough but possible.

## When It Is Impossible

If the math gives a number over 100%, your target is mathematically impossible. If it gives a number below 0%, you have already secured your target grade.
