# Final Exam Grade Calculator: Predict Your Course Grade

Before walking into your final exam, you should know exactly how it will affect your final grade. A final exam grade calculator lets you model different scenarios so you know what score secures your target grade.

## How Weighted Grades Work

Most courses break grades into categories with different weights:
- Homework: 20%
- Midterm: 30%
- Final exam: 40%
- Participation: 10%

For more on this topic, see [*How to Raise Your GPA Fast: Practical Strategies That Actually Work*](/blog/how-to-raise-your-gpa-fast).

Your final grade is the weighted average of these categories.

## Example Calculation

| Category | Weight | Your Score | Weighted Contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homework | 20% | 88% | 17.6 |
| Midterm | 30% | 82% | 24.6 |
| Participation | 10% | 95% | 9.5 |
| Final Exam | 40% | Unknown | 40% |

Current weighted total without final: 51.7

To get an A (90%): (90 - 51.7) / 0.40 = **95.75% needed on final**
To get a B (80%): (80 - 51.7) / 0.40 = **70.75% needed on final**

## What If My Grades Are Points, Not Percentages?

The math works the same. Convert everything to percentages first. If homework is worth 200 points and you earned 180, your percentage is 90%.

## Planning Your Study Time

If the calculator shows you need 95% for an A but only 65% to pass, make a strategic decision. Maybe an A is not worth the stress, and a B+ frees up time for another class where you are borderline.
