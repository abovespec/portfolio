# Best Free Website Monitoring Tools Compared (2025)

Website monitoring catches problems before customers do. The good news is that excellent free monitoring tools exist. This guide compares the best options and helps you choose the right stack for your needs.

## Uptime Monitoring

### UptimeRobot (Free Tier)
- 50 monitors
- 5-minute intervals
- Email alerts
- HTTP, ping, port, and keyword monitoring
- Best for: Small businesses and personal sites

### Freshping (Free Tier)
- 50 monitors
- 1-minute intervals
- Email and SMS alerts
- Public status pages
- Best for: Teams needing faster alerting

### Better Uptime (Free Tier)
- 10 monitors
- 3-minute intervals
- Beautiful status pages
- Slack and Teams integrations
- Best for: Startups wanting polished status pages

## Performance Monitoring

### GTmetrix
- Free daily tests
- Page speed scores
- Waterfall charts
- Best for: Tracking performance over time

### Google PageSpeed Insights
- Unlimited tests
- Core Web Vitals data
- Mobile and desktop scores
- Best for: SEO-related performance

### WebPageTest
- Unlimited tests
- Advanced diagnostics
- Multiple locations and devices
- Best for: Deep technical analysis

## SSL and Domain Monitoring

### SSL Labs Test
- Free SSL certificate analysis
- Grade and vulnerability detection
- Best for: Quarterly SSL audits

### DomainIQ or WHOIS
- Domain expiration tracking
- Best for: Preventing accidental lapses

## Error Tracking

### Sentry (Free Tier)
- 5,000 errors/month
- Stack traces and context
- Best for: Web applications

### Rollbar (Free Tier)
- 5,000 events/month
- Real-time error alerts
- Best for: Development teams

- You require phone call alerts
- You need synthetic transaction monitoring
