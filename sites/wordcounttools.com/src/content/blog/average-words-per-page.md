"Write 5 pages" is an unclear instruction unless you know the font, font size, line spacing, and margins. Here's what the standard formatting choices yield.

## Standard academic formatting

The most common academic format in North America is:

- **Font:** Times New Roman or Arial, 12pt
- **Spacing:** Double-spaced
- **Margins:** 1 inch on all sides
- **Page size:** US Letter (8.5 × 11 in)

Under these conditions: **approximately 250–275 words per page.**

| Pages | Approximate word count |
|-------|------------------------|
| 1 | 250–275 |
| 2 | 500–550 |
| 3 | 750–825 |
| 5 | 1,250–1,375 |
| 10 | 2,500–2,750 |
| 20 | 5,000–5,500 |

These are approximations. The actual count varies based on how many paragraphs end mid-page, heading placement, and dialogue formatting (which leaves more white space).

## Single-spaced pages

Single-spaced documents at 12pt Times New Roman, 1-inch margins: **approximately 500 words per page.**

| Spacing | Words per page (approx.) |
|---------|--------------------------|
| Double-spaced | 250–275 |
| 1.5-spaced | 375 |
| Single-spaced | 500 |

## Font size matters

| Font size | Words per page (double-spaced) |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| 10pt | ~330 |
| 11pt | ~290 |
| 12pt | ~250 |
| 14pt | ~210 |

Smaller fonts fit more words; larger fonts fewer. If your assignment doesn't specify, 12pt Times New Roman double-spaced is the default academic standard.

## Fiction publishing

Published fiction uses different formatting than academic papers. A standard manuscript page (the format authors submit to publishers) uses:

- Courier 12pt (monospaced)
- Double-spaced
- 1-inch margins
- **~250 words per page** (publishers prefer Courier because it's predictable)

A finished published novel is typeset differently. A typical trade paperback has about 300–350 words per page. A mass-market paperback (smaller trim size, smaller margins, sometimes 10pt type) can reach 350–450 words per page.

## Word count targets for different writing lengths

| Format | Standard length | Pages (double-spaced) |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| Flash fiction | Under 1,000 words | ~4 |
| Short story | 1,000–7,500 words | 4–30 |
| Novelette | 7,500–17,500 words | 30–70 |
| Novella | 17,500–40,000 words | 70–160 |
| Novel (genre) | 70,000–90,000 words | 280–360 |
| Novel (literary) | 80,000–100,000 words | 320–400 |
| Epic fantasy | 100,000–200,000+ words | 400–800+ |

*These are industry norms, not hard limits. First novels are typically held to tighter ranges.*

## College essays and applications

| Application | Word limit |
|-------------|-----------|
| Common App main essay | 250–650 words |
| Common App activities | 150 words |
| Supplemental essays | 50–650 words (varies by school) |
| UC Personal Insight Questions | 250–350 words each |
| Coalition App | 500–650 words |
| SAT Essay (discontinued 2021) | — |

For college essays, hitting the word limit isn't optional — it's part of demonstrating you can follow directions and be concise. Aim to be within 10% of the maximum.

## Business and professional documents

| Document | Typical length |
|----------|---------------|
| Email | 50–200 words |
| Executive summary | 200–400 words |
| Business proposal | 2,000–10,000 words |
| One-page resume | ~400–600 words |
| Press release | 400–500 words |
| Technical white paper | 2,500–10,000 words |
| Case study | 800–1,500 words |

## Converting page counts to word counts

Quick formula:

- Double-spaced, 12pt: pages × 250
- Single-spaced, 12pt: pages × 500
- Published book page: pages × 300

Online word count tools like [wordcounttools.com](/) show live word count alongside page count estimates, so you can target either metric.
