Sales tax is a percentage of the purchase price added at the point of sale. Knowing how to calculate it manually means you can verify receipts, estimate totals before reaching the checkout, and figure out the pre-tax price when shopping online.

## How sales tax works

Sales tax is calculated as a percentage of the item's price. The rate is set by local government — it varies by state, county, and sometimes city. The tax is added on top of the listed price, so the total you pay is:

For more on this topic, see [*Percentage of a Number: What It Means and How to Calculate It*](/blog/percentage-of-a-number).

```
Total = Price × (1 + Tax Rate / 100)
```

Or broken into two steps:

```
Tax Amount = Price × (Tax Rate / 100)
Total = Price + Tax Amount
```

## Calculating sales tax on a purchase

### Example 1 — Basic purchase

A book costs $24.99. The sales tax rate is 6%. How much tax do you pay, and what is the total?

```
Tax = $24.99 × (6 / 100) = $24.99 × 0.06 = $1.499 ≈ $1.50
Total = $24.99 + $1.50 = $26.49
```

### Example 2 — Electronics purchase with combined rate

A laptop is priced at $849. The combined state and local tax rate is 8.75%.

```
Tax = $849 × 0.0875 = $74.29
Total = $849 + $74.29 = $923.29
```

### Example 3 — Multiple items

You buy three items: $12.50, $34.00, and $7.75. The tax rate is 7%.

1. Add up the pre-tax total: $12.50 + $34.00 + $7.75 = $54.25
2. Calculate tax: $54.25 × 0.07 = $3.80
3. Total: $54.25 + $3.80 = $58.05

## Finding the tax rate from a receipt

If your receipt shows the pre-tax price and the tax amount, you can calculate the rate that was applied:

For more on this topic, see [*How to Calculate a Discount Percentage (and the Final Price)*](/blog/how-to-calculate-discount).

```
Tax Rate % = (Tax Amount / Pre-Tax Price) × 100
```

### Example

Your receipt shows: subtotal $45.00, tax $3.15, total $48.15.

```
Tax Rate = ($3.15 / $45.00) × 100 = 7%
```

This is useful for verifying that the correct rate was applied to your purchase.

## Finding the pre-tax price from the total

Sometimes you only have the final total and want to know what the pre-tax price was. This is common when comparing prices across different states or checking whether a quote includes tax.

```
Pre-Tax Price = Total / (1 + Tax Rate / 100)
```

### Example

A receipt shows a total of $107.00 and you know the tax rate was 7%. What was the pre-tax price?

```
Pre-Tax Price = $107.00 / 1.07 = $100.00
```

### Example — Tip calculation on a restaurant bill

Your restaurant total including 8% sales tax is $86.40. You want to calculate your tip on the pre-tax amount (a common practice):

```
Pre-Tax = $86.40 / 1.08 = $80.00
15% tip on $80 = $12.00
20% tip on $80 = $16.00
```

## Combined state and local tax rates

In the United States, sales tax rates are not uniform — the rate you pay is typically the state rate plus any county or city additions. For example:

- State rate: 6.0%
- County addition: 1.0%
- City addition: 0.75%
- **Combined rate: 7.75%**

The formula remains identical regardless of how the combined rate is made up. You use the single total percentage in the calculation.

For more on this topic, see [*How to Calculate Percentage: Formula, Examples, and Shortcuts*](/blog/how-to-calculate-percentage).

## Tax on tax-exempt items

Many jurisdictions exempt certain goods from sales tax — typically groceries, prescription medications, and some clothing. In practice this means you apply the tax rate only to the taxable portion of your purchase.

### Example

You buy:
- Groceries: $62.00 (exempt)
- Household supplies: $31.50 (taxable)
- Tax rate: 6.5%

```
Tax = $31.50 × 0.065 = $2.05
Total = $62.00 + $31.50 + $2.05 = $95.55
```

## Quick mental math for common tax rates

**6% tax:** Find 5% (half of 10%), then add 1%.
- 5% of $80 = $4.00
- 1% of $80 = $0.80
- 6% = $4.80

**8% tax:** Find 10%, subtract 2%.
- 10% of $50 = $5.00
- 2% of $50 = $1.00
- 8% = $4.00

**10% tax:** Move the decimal one place left. Instant.

**7.5% tax:** Find 7%, add half of 1%.
- 7% of $60 = $4.20
- 0.5% of $60 = $0.30
- 7.5% = $4.50

## VAT vs. sales tax

If you are shopping internationally, note that VAT (Value Added Tax) works differently from sales tax in how it is collected — it is applied at each stage of production rather than only at retail. However, from a consumer's perspective, the calculation to find the VAT-inclusive price or extract the pre-tax price is identical:

```
Price including VAT = Pre-VAT price × (1 + VAT rate / 100)
Pre-VAT price = Total / (1 + VAT rate / 100)
```

## Summary of formulas

| What you want | Formula |
|---|---|
| Tax amount | `Price × (Tax Rate / 100)` |
| Total including tax | `Price × (1 + Tax Rate / 100)` |
| Tax rate from receipt | `(Tax Amount / Pre-Tax Price) × 100` |
| Pre-tax price from total | `Total / (1 + Tax Rate / 100)` |
