## Percent Off Calculator — Final Price After a Discount

Enter the original price and the discount percentage to see your savings and the final price.

### The Formula

**Savings = Original Price × (Discount% ÷ 100)**

**Final Price = Original Price × (1 − Discount% ÷ 100)**

### Examples

| Original Price | Discount | You Save | Final Price |
|----------------|----------|----------|-------------|
| $120.00 | 20% off | $24.00 | $96.00 |
| $49.99 | 15% off | $7.50 | $42.49 |
| $250.00 | 33% off | $82.50 | $167.50 |

### Stacked Discounts

Two sequential discounts of 20% and 10% combine to **28% off**, not 30%:
`1 − (1 − 0.20)(1 − 0.10) = 28%`
