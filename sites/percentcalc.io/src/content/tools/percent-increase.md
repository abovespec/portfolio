## Percent Increase Calculator — New Value After a Percentage Increase

### The Formula

**New Value = Original × (1 + Increase% ÷ 100)**

### Common Uses

**Salary raise.** $65,000 salary + 4.5% raise = $65,000 × 1.045 = **$67,925**.

**Price increase.** $29.99 + 12% = $29.99 × 1.12 = **$33.59**.

**Investment growth.** $10,000 at 7% for one year = **$10,700**.

### Compounding Increases

For repeated increases: **Final = Start × (1 + Rate)^n**

$10,000 at 7%/year for 10 years = $10,000 × (1.07)^10 ≈ **$19,672**.
