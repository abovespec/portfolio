# Flooring Cost Calculator by Square Foot: Material and Installation

Flooring is one of the highest-impact home improvements. It is also one of the easiest to miscalculate. This guide breaks down flooring costs by material, includes the hidden expenses, and shows how to estimate your total project cost by square footage.

## Material Costs Per Square Foot

| Material | Material Only | Installed |
|---|---|---|
| Carpet | $2-5 | $4-8 |
| Laminate | $1-4 | $3-7 |
| Vinyl Plank (LVP) | $2-5 | $4-8 |
| Engineered Hardwood | $4-8 | $7-12 |
| Solid Hardwood | $5-12 | $9-18 |
| Tile (Ceramic) | $1-5 | $6-12 |
| Tile (Porcelain) | $3-8 | $8-15 |
| Natural Stone | $8-20 | $15-30 |
| Bamboo | $3-6 | $6-10 |
| Cork | $3-6 | $6-10 |

## Hidden Costs

- **Underlayment:** $0.30-1.00 per sq ft
- **Moisture barrier:** $0.20-0.50 per sq ft
- **Transition strips:** $10-30 each
- **Quarter round / base shoe:** $1-3 per linear foot
- **Floor prep / leveling:** $1-3 per sq ft
- **Removal of old flooring:** $1-4 per sq ft
- **Waste factor:** Add 10% to square footage

## Example: 200 Sq Ft Bedroom, LVP

- Area: 200 sq ft
- With 10% waste: 220 sq ft
- LVP material: 220 x $4 = $880
- Underlayment: 220 x $0.50 = $110
- Installation: 200 x $3 = $600
- Transitions and trim: $100
- **Total: approximately $1,690**

## DIY vs Professional

DIY saves 30-50% on labor but requires tools, time, and skill. Floating floors (laminate, LVP) are DIY-friendly. Tile and hardwood usually need professional installation for warranty and durability.

## Tips to Save Money

- Buy discontinued colors at clearance prices.
- Remove old flooring yourself.
- Paint baseboards instead of replacing them.
- Shop during holiday sales (Memorial Day, Labor Day, Black Friday).
