# How Much Paint Do I Need for a 12x12 Room? Exact Calculation

A 12x12 room is the most common bedroom size, yet paint quantity estimates vary wildly online. This guide gives you the exact calculation, explains the variables, and shows how to use a paint calculator for any room.

## Basic Room Dimensions

- Floor: 12 ft x 12 ft = 144 sq ft
- Wall height: 8 ft standard, 9-10 ft common in newer homes
- Total wall area (8 ft ceiling): 2 x (12+12) x 8 = 384 sq ft
- Ceiling: 144 sq ft
- Total paintable surface: 528 sq ft

## Subtract Non-Paintable Areas

- Standard window: 15 sq ft each
- Standard door: 21 sq ft each
- Baseboard and trim: usually painted separately

A 12x12 room with two windows and one door:
- Wall area: 384 - 30 - 21 = 333 sq ft
- Ceiling: 144 sq ft

## Paint Coverage

One gallon covers approximately 350-400 sq ft per coat on smooth walls. Textured walls or dark color coverage drops to 300 sq ft per gallon.

## Gallons Needed

**Walls only, two coats:**
- 333 sq ft x 2 coats = 666 sq ft
- 666 / 350 = 1.9 gallons
- Round up: **2 gallons**

**Ceiling, two coats:**
- 144 sq ft x 2 = 288 sq ft
- 288 / 400 = 0.72 gallons
- Round up: **1 gallon**

**Total: 3 gallons** for walls and ceiling.

## Variables That Change the Math

- **Dark colors over light:** May need three coats.
- **Textured walls:** Reduce coverage by 15-25%.
- **Drastic color change:** Budget 25% more paint.
- **New drywall:** Absorbs more paint. Prime first.

## Trim Paint

For baseboards, door frames, and window trim in a 12x12 room:
- Linear feet of trim: approximately 60-70 feet
- Coverage: 400 linear feet per gallon
- **1 quart** usually covers all trim with two coats.

## Cost Estimate

- 3 gallons wall paint at $40/gallon: $120
- 1 gallon ceiling paint at $35/gallon: $35
- 1 quart trim paint at $20: $20
- Total material cost: approximately **$175**
