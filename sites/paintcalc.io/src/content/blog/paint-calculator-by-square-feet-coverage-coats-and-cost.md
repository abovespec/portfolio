# Paint Calculator by Square Feet: Coverage, Coats, and Cost

Professional painters estimate by square footage because it is the most reliable method. This guide explains how to calculate paint needs from square footage, what coverage rates to use, and how to adjust for real-world conditions.

## Standard Coverage Rates

| Surface Type | Sq Ft per Gallon (1 coat) |
|---|---|
| Smooth interior walls | 350-400 |
| Textured walls | 250-300 |
| Fresh drywall (primed) | 350-400 |
| Fresh drywall (unprimed) | 200-250 |
| Ceiling (smooth) | 400-450 |
| Ceiling (textured) | 300-350 |
| Trim and doors | 300-400 |

## The Calculation

1. Measure total square footage to paint.
2. Divide by coverage rate for your surface.
3. Multiply by number of coats.
4. Round up to the nearest quart or gallon.

For more on this topic, see [*Roofing Square Footage Calculator: Shingles, Bundles, and Cost*](/blog/roofing-square-footage-calculator-shingles-bundles-and-cost).

**Example:** 500 sq ft of smooth walls, two coats.
- 500 / 375 = 1.33 gallons per coat
- 1.33 x 2 = 2.66 gallons
- Round up: **3 gallons**

## Coat Adjustments

- **Same color refresh:** One coat often sufficient.
- **Light over dark:** Two coats minimum.
- **Dark over light:** Two coats, possibly three for deep reds or blues.
- **Primer + paint:** One coat primer, one coat paint on previously painted surfaces.

## Buying Strategy

Always round up. It is better to have a quart left over than to run out mid-wall. Paint stores can tint quarts to match if you need more later, but slight batch variations exist.

## Cost Per Square Foot

- Economy paint: $0.08-0.12 per sq ft per coat
- Mid-grade paint: $0.12-0.18 per sq ft per coat
- Premium paint: $0.18-0.30 per sq ft per coat

For a 12x12 room with two coats using mid-grade paint:
- 384 sq ft walls x 2 coats = 768 sq ft
- At $0.15/sq ft: $115 in paint
