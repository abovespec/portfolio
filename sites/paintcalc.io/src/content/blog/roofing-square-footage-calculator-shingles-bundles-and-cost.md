# Roofing Square Footage Calculator: Shingles, Bundles, and Cost

Roofing estimates confuse homeowners because the roof surface is larger than the house footprint. Pitch, overhangs, and valleys all add area. This guide explains how to calculate roofing square footage accurately and convert it into material needs.

## Why Roof Area Exceeds Floor Area

A pitched roof has more surface area than the flat footprint below. The steeper the pitch, the more surface area. A 6:12 pitch adds about 12% to the footprint. A 12:12 pitch adds about 41%.

## The Calculation

1. Measure house footprint (length x width).
2. Add overhangs (typically 1-2 feet per side).
3. Multiply by pitch factor.

**Pitch factors:**
- Flat to 3:12: 1.00 to 1.03
- 4:12: 1.05
- 6:12: 1.12
- 8:12: 1.20
- 10:12: 1.30
- 12:12: 1.41

## Example: 30x40 Foot House, 6:12 Pitch

- Footprint: 30 x 40 = 1,200 sq ft
- With 1-foot overhangs: 32 x 42 = 1,344 sq ft
- With 6:12 pitch: 1,344 x 1.12 = 1,505 sq ft roof area

## Roofing Squares

Roofers measure in "squares." One square = 100 sq ft.

- 1,505 sq ft = 15.05 squares
- Round up: **16 squares**

## Shingle Bundles

Three bundles of standard asphalt shingles cover one square.

- 16 squares x 3 bundles = **48 bundles**
- Add 10% waste: **53 bundles**

## Cost Estimate

- Architectural shingles: $100-150 per square
- 16 squares at $125: $2,000
- Underlayment: $400
- Flashing and vents: $300
- Labor (if hired): $200-400 per square
- **DIY total: ~$2,700**
- **Professional total: ~$5,500-9,000**
