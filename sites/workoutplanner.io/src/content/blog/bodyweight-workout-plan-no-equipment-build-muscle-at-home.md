# Bodyweight Workout Plan No Equipment: Build Muscle at Home

You do not need a gym to build strength. A well-designed bodyweight workout plan can develop muscle, improve endurance, and increase mobility using only the resistance of your own body. This guide provides a complete no-equipment routine for all fitness levels.

## Why Bodyweight Training Works

Bodyweight exercises are closed-chain movements, meaning your hands or feet remain fixed while your body moves. This often produces better joint stability and functional strength than machine-based open-chain exercises.

Progressive overload is still possible. You increase difficulty by changing leverage, adding reps, slowing tempo, or reducing rest periods.

For more on this topic, see [*How to Progressive Overload at Home: No Gym Required*](/blog/how-to-progressive-overload-at-home-no-gym-required).

## The Beginner Routine (3 days/week)

**Day 1 - Push Focus:**
- Push-ups: 3 sets x max reps
- Pike push-ups: 3 sets x 8-12
- Tricep dips (using a chair): 3 sets x 10-15
- Plank: 3 sets x 30 seconds

**Day 2 - Pull and Legs:**
- Doorframe rows or towel rows: 3 sets x 10-15
- Superman holds: 3 sets x 20 seconds
- Bodyweight squats: 3 sets x 15-20
- Reverse lunges: 3 sets x 10 per leg
- Glute bridges: 3 sets x 15-20

**Day 3 - Full Body:**
- Burpees: 3 sets x 8-12
- Mountain climbers: 3 sets x 20 seconds
- Bear crawls: 3 sets x 30 seconds
- Dead bug: 3 sets x 10 per side
- Wall sit: 3 sets x 30-45 seconds

For more on this topic, see [*3 Day Workout Split for Beginners: Full Body Strength Plan*](/blog/3-day-workout-split-for-beginners-full-body-strength-plan).

## Progressions for Each Movement

**Push-ups:**
- Wall push-up -> Incline push-up -> Standard push-up -> Diamond push-up -> Archer push-up -> One-arm push-up

**Squats:**
- Assisted squat -> Bodyweight squat -> Split squat -> Bulgarian split squat -> Pistol squat

**Rows:**
- Doorframe row -> Towel row -> Horizontal row (under table) -> Australian row (low bar)

## Advanced Bodyweight Routine

For those who have mastered basics:
- Muscle-up progression
- Front lever progression
- Planche progression
- One-arm push-up
- Pistol squat
- Handstand push-up

## Nutrition Without a Gym

Bodyweight training builds muscle if you eat enough protein. Target 0.7-1 gram per pound of body weight. A slight calorie surplus supports growth. Sleep 7-9 hours for recovery.

For more on this topic, see [*4 Day Workout Split for Muscle Growth: Hypertrophy Program*](/blog/4-day-workout-split-muscle-growth).

## Common Mistakes

- **Skipping pull movements:** Find a way to do rows. Push-only routines create imbalances.
- **Rushing reps:** Slow, controlled reps build more muscle than fast, sloppy reps.
- **Never progressing:** Add difficulty every 1-2 weeks.
- **Inconsistent training:** Three sessions weekly minimum.

## The Bottom Line

Bodyweight training is limited only by creativity and discipline. Gymnasts build world-class physiques with no external weights. You can build an impressive physique in your living room with the right plan.
