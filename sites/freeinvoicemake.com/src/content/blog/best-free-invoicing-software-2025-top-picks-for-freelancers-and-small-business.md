# Best Free Invoicing Software 2025: Top Picks for Freelancers and Small Business

You do not need to pay for invoicing when you are starting out. Several excellent free options exist. This comparison covers the best free invoicing software in 2025, with honest assessments of their limitations.

## 1. Wave (Completely Free)

Wave offers unlimited invoicing, accounting, and receipt scanning at no cost. It makes money through optional payment processing and payroll. The interface is clean and designed for small business owners, not accountants.

**Best for:** Freelancers and small businesses in the US and Canada.
**Limitation:** No multi-currency support on the free plan.

## 2. Zoho Invoice (Free Plan)

Zoho Invoice is free forever for up to 1,000 customers. It includes time tracking, expense logging, and multiple currencies. If you outgrow it, upgrading to Zoho Books is seamless.

**Best for:** International freelancers and growing agencies.
**Limitation:** 1,000 customer cap.

## 3. Stripe Invoicing (Free to Send)

Stripe Invoicing charges no fee to create and send invoices. You only pay Stripe's standard payment processing fee when you get paid. It is simple, fast, and integrates with any Stripe-connected business.

**Best for:** Businesses already using Stripe.
**Limitation:** Basic feature set compared to full accounting tools.

## 4. PayPal Invoicing (Free to Send)

Similar to Stripe, PayPal charges nothing to send invoices. Fees apply only when payments come in. Most clients already have PayPal, which reduces payment friction.

**Best for:** Clients who prefer PayPal.
**Limitation:** Higher processing fees than Stripe in some regions.

## 5. Our Free Invoice Generator

No account, no limits, no cost. Enter details, generate, print. Perfect for occasional invoices or when you need something immediately.

**Best for:** One-off invoices and quick turnaround.
**Limitation:** No payment tracking or recurring billing.
