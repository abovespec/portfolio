## What a Good Invoice Needs

Every professional invoice contains:
- Your business name and contact information
- Client name and billing address
- Unique invoice number
- Invoice date and due date
- Line items with descriptions, quantities, and rates
- Subtotal, tax, and total
- Payment terms and methods

## When to Upgrade to Invoicing Software

A simple generator works for freelancers with occasional invoices. Consider dedicated software when you:
- Send more than 10 invoices monthly
- Need recurring billing
- Want payment tracking and reminders
- Require multi-currency support
- Have multiple team members creating invoices

## Invoice Best Practices

- Number invoices sequentially starting at 001 or 1001.
- Set due dates within 14 days for new clients, 30 days for established relationships.
- Include your preferred payment method and any late fee policy.
- Send invoices within 48 hours of project completion.

According to [Intuit's invoicing guide](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/invoicing/), consistent invoicing habits — including prompt delivery and clear payment instructions — are among the most effective ways to reduce average days to payment.
