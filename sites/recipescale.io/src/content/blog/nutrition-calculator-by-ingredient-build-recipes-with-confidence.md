# Nutrition Calculator by Ingredient: Build Recipes with Confidence

Homemade meals are healthier than restaurant food, but only if you know what is in them. A nutrition calculator by ingredient lets you build recipes, see exact macros, and make informed adjustments. This guide explains how to analyze recipes nutritionally.

## Why Ingredient-Level Analysis Matters

Generic nutrition databases assign averages. Your "chicken breast" might be 4 oz or 8 oz. Your "tablespoon of olive oil" might be a heavy pour. Ingredient-level calculators use the exact weight and brand of each item.

## The Process

1. List every ingredient in your recipe
2. Enter the exact weight or volume for each
3. The calculator looks up USDA or branded nutrition data
4. It sums calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, and sodium
5. Divide by servings for per-serving nutrition

## Adjusting for Health Goals

**To reduce calories:** Cut oil by half, use a sugar substitute, reduce nut portions.
**To increase protein:** Add protein powder, Greek yogurt, or hemp seeds.
**To reduce sodium:** Use fresh ingredients instead of canned or processed items.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Forgetting cooking oil:** Adds 100-200 calories per serving.
- **Ignoring marinades:** The full marinade counts even if some is discarded.
- **Eyeballing portions:** A kitchen scale is more accurate than volume measures.
- **Using raw vs cooked weights:** Meat loses 25-30% weight when cooked.
