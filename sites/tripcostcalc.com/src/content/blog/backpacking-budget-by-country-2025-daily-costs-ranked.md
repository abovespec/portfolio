# Backpacking Budget by Country 2025: Daily Costs Ranked

Backpacking is the cheapest way to see the world, but daily costs vary enormously by country. A day in Thailand costs less than a dinner in Switzerland. This guide ranks backpacking budgets by country and region for 2025.

## Methodology

Our daily estimates include:
- Hostel dorm bed or budget guesthouse
- Street food and one casual restaurant meal
- Local transport (buses, metro, shared taxis)
- One paid attraction or activity
- A small contingency

They do not include intercity transport or alcohol, which vary by itinerary and personal choice. Daily cost ranges are cross-referenced with [Budget Your Trip](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/) and [Numbeo cost of living data](https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/).

## Stretching Your Budget

- Cook your own meals when possible
- Use overnight buses/trains to save accommodation
- Walk instead of taking taxis
- Stay in hostel dorms, not private rooms
- Travel in shoulder or low season
- Use local SIM cards instead of roaming
