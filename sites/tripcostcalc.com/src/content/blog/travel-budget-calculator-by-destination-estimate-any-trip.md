## The 10% Rule

Add 10% to every budget for unexpected expenses. Missed flights, medical needs, and spontaneous experiences happen. A buffer removes financial stress from your trip.

## Saving Money Without Sacrificing Experience

- Travel in shoulder season
- Eat where locals eat
- Use public transit
- Book accommodations with kitchens
- Take overnight buses/trains to save a hotel night
- Use free walking tours for orientation

## The Bottom Line

Budgeting does not restrict travel. It enables it. When you know your numbers, you can say yes to experiences confidently. Use our calculator to plan your next adventure.
